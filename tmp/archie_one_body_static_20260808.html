    Corpus → weights

    Books, documents, and crawled records are material inputs with provenance, rights, deduplication, normalization, held-out separation, sampling, gradients, and receipts. “The model ate a book” is not an admissible description.

    01AcquireFiles, books, scans, public records, repositories.source
    02ExtractDecode / OCR / parse into machine-readable records.record
    03FilterRights, normalization, dedup, mixture metadata.corpus
    04TokenizeFinite symbol sequence.u16
    05SampleWindows, boundaries, train/eval split.batch
    06UpdateNext-byte CE → gradients → optimizer → parameters.θ
    Project Panama: keep the acquisition/digitization boundary explicit; do not infer OCR, filtering, mixture, tokenizer, or training schedule from evidence of physical scanning alone.
