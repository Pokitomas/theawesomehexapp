Framing
§ 0 — GROUND RULES

Two failure modes, deliberately avoided

Premature synthesis is the reflex that ends every discussion of singularities with “and then quantum gravity takes over.” That sentence is a coupon for a theory nobody has, and it gets spent long before classical general relativity has been exhausted. The classical theory has more to say about the terminus than it is usually allowed to say.

Tunnel vision is building all intuition on Schwarzschild and Oppenheimer–Snyder, which are the two least generic solutions in the problem. Section 4 measures exactly how non-generic, with a one-line algebraic condition.

The organising move is the third thing: take the time coordinate to be arbitrary and see which statements survive. Statements that don’t survive aren’t physics — they’re bookkeeping about a foliation. That filter does most of the work below.

Established
§ 1 — WHAT 1939 AND 1965 ACTUALLY SAY

The theorem concludes incompleteness, and nothing else

Oppenheimer & Snyder matched a closed dust FLRW interior to an exterior Schwarzschild and got two results, both correct, both about curves: a comoving dust element reaches zero areal radius in finite proper time, and a distant observer never sees the surface cross r = 2M — the luminosity fades with e-folding time \(3\sqrt{3}\,M\), which is 25.59 μs for a solar mass.

They flagged the zero-volume endpoint as an artefact of pressureless dust. The standard reply is that Penrose closed that loophole. The reply is right, and it is usually misread.

Penrose 1965

If a spacetime admits a non-compact Cauchy surface, satisfies the null convergence condition \(R_{ab}k^ak^b \ge 0\), and contains a closed trapped surface, then it is future null geodesically incomplete.

Read the conclusion carefully. It is not “the density becomes infinite.” It is not “there is a point where matter is destroyed.” It is: some inextendible causal geodesic has finite affine length. The theorem is silent about what sits at the end — and the silence is structural, because its hypotheses are global and causal while “what happens there” is local and metric.

Three consequences get skipped routinely:

  • The singularity is not a set. It is a property of the manifold. Every attempt to attach it as a boundary has known pathologies — Schmidt’s b-boundary puts the singular point in the closure of every interior point for closed FRW and for Schwarzschild, so the quotient topology isn’t even T₁. Only the causal boundary is in decent shape, and it keeps causal but not metric information.
  • Curvature need not diverge. Quasi-regular “whimper” singularities are geodesically incomplete with bounded curvature. Divergence is an extra fact about Schwarzschild, not a corollary.
  • Incomplete does not mean inextendible. This one breaks the standard story outright — §6.

§ 2 — THE ARBITRARY-TIME FILTER

“When does the matter vanish?” has no invariant answer

Define the survival functional of a foliation. Let \(\mu\) be the measure on the matter congruence and \(\Sigma_t\) any global spacelike foliation:

Survival functional

\[ N_\Sigma(t) \;=\; \mu\!\left\{\, \text{worldlines meeting } \Sigma_t \,\right\} \]

The formal object hiding behind every sentence of the form “the matter is gone.” It is not a diffeomorphism invariant, and the failure is not subtle.

In Painlevé–Gullstrand or ingoing Eddington–Finkelstein slicing, the slices are regular across the horizon and run into r = 0. \(N_\Sigma(t)\) drops to zero at finite \(t\); matter “vanishes.”

In maximal slicing (\(K=0\)) the lapse obeys \(\nabla^2\alpha = \alpha K_{ij}K^{ij}\), so by the maximum principle \(\alpha > 0\) everywhere but collapses in the strong field. The slices are limited by the double root of \(f r^4 + C^2\) with \(f = 1-2M/r\):

Limiting maximal slice

\[ r_0 = \tfrac{3}{2}M, \qquad C = \tfrac{3\sqrt{3}}{4}M^2, \qquad R_{abcd}R^{abcd}\big|_{r_0} = \frac{1024}{243\,M^4} \]

Finite curvature. The foliation asymptotes to an eternal cylinder outside r = 0 and never reaches it — the “trumpet” every moving-puncture code lives on. In this time, \(N_\Sigma(t) = N_0\) forever: the collapse never completes and no matter ever disappears.

Both foliations are global, spacelike, asymptotically flat, and cover the entire exterior. They disagree about whether the matter still exists.

“Matter vanishes upon reaching the end of its worldline” is a statement about a choice of time function, not about the spacetime. The invariant residue is only: these curves are incomplete, and here is the limiting behaviour along each.

That residue is computable. The rest of this note computes it.

§ 3 — WHAT THE TERMINUS DOES TO A BODY

Zero volume, unbounded length

Radial marginally bound infall gives \(r(s) = (9M/2)^{1/3}s^{2/3}\), so the tidal coefficient is universal — every trace of \(M\) cancels:

Tidal coefficient and deviation exponents

\[ \frac{M}{r^3} \;=\; \frac{2}{9s^2} \]

Geodesic deviation in the parallel-propagated frame, \(\ddot\xi^{\hat i} = -R^{\hat i}{}_{\hat t \hat j \hat t}\,\xi^{\hat j}\), is then an Euler equation:

\[ \text{transverse: } p(p-1) = -\tfrac{2}{9} \;\Rightarrow\; p \in \{\tfrac13, \tfrac23\} \] \[ \text{radial: } p(p-1) = +\tfrac{4}{9} \;\Rightarrow\; p \in \{-\tfrac13, \tfrac43\} \]

Reading the metric directly confirms it: near r = 0 the vacuum interior is Kasner with exponents \((-\tfrac13, \tfrac23, \tfrac23)\), satisfying \(\sum p = \sum p^2 = 1\). So the body arrives stretched to unbounded length and squeezed to zero cross-section, with three-volume going to zero. Not crushed to a point. “Zero volume” and “crushed to a point” are different claims, and only the first is true.

Two more numbers worth having. The longest proper time any worldline has between the horizon and the terminus is

Lorentzian diameter of the interior

\[ \int_0^{2M}\frac{\sqrt{r}\;dr}{\sqrt{2M-r}} \;=\; \pi M \qquad (15.47\;\mu\text{s for one solar mass}) \]

— so the interior is a Lorentzian metric space of finite diameter, which matters in §8. And in the Kasner parametrisation \(p_1 = -u/(1{+}u{+}u^2)\), \(p_2 = (1{+}u)/(1{+}u{+}u^2)\), \(p_3 = u(1{+}u)/(1{+}u{+}u^2)\), Schwarzschild sits at exactly \(u = 1\) — the locally rotationally symmetric point, the fixed point of the BKL era map, the one Kasner that does not oscillate.

The canonical black hole interior sits at the single most degenerate point of the space of terminal geometries.

§ 4 — THE CENTREPIECE

The 1939 endpoint is not merely unphysical. It is Weyl-free.

Oppenheimer and Snyder said dust is an idealisation. Here is a sharper and more damaging statement: even inside dust, their endpoint is a measure-zero degeneracy — and it degenerates in the one direction that inverts its physical meaning.

Take marginally bound Lemaître–Tolman–Bondi dust. Same matter, same symmetry, one free function \(m(r)\) instead of a constant density:

LTB dust, expanded about the terminus

\[ \dot R^2 = \frac{2m(r)}{R}, \qquad R(\tau,r) = \left[\, r^{3/2} - \tfrac{3}{2}\sqrt{2m}\;\tau \,\right]^{2/3} \]

Each shell crunches at its own comoving time \(\tau_s(r) = \tfrac23 r^{3/2}/\sqrt{2m}\). Setting \(s = \tau_s(r) - \tau\) and expanding as \(s \to 0\):

\[ R \;\to\; \left(\tfrac{9m}{2}\right)^{1/3} s^{2/3} \] \[ R' \;\to\; \left[\frac{2^{1/6}\,3^{2/3}\sqrt{r}\,\bigl(3m - r\,m'\bigr)}{9\,m^{7/6}}\right] s^{-1/3} \]
The entire Oppenheimer–Snyder special case is the vanishing of one algebraic factor: \(3m - r\,m' = 0\), that is, \(m \propto r^3\), that is, uniform density.

Off that locus — for every other mass profile — the radial scale factor goes as \(s^{-1/3}\), the angular ones as \(s^{2/3}\), and the terminus is the Kasner of §3. Generic dust spaghettifies. Uniform dust does not. The difference is one function’s worth of fine-tuning.

Now split the curvature

Separate the Ricci (matter) part from the Weyl (free gravitational field) part. For LTB the single Weyl scalar is \(\Psi_2 = m/R^3 - m'/(3R^2R')\), and the two pieces scale differently:

Ricci against Weyl at the terminus

\[ \frac{m}{R^3} \;\longrightarrow\; \frac{2}{9s^2} \qquad\text{— independent of } m(r) \text{ entirely} \] \[ \rho = \frac{m'}{4\pi R^2 R'} \;\sim\; s^{-1} \]

(a) The generic terminus is Weyl-dominated, and universally so. The ratio Ricci/Weyl is \(O(s) \to 0\): the collapsing matter’s own energy density becomes dynamically irrelevant at its own terminus. And the surviving Weyl scalar \(2/9s^2\) carries no memory of \(m(r)\) — every dust profile approaches the same terminal geometry.

(b) The Oppenheimer–Snyder terminus has \(\Psi_2 \equiv 0\) exactly. Its interior is FLRW, hence conformally flat, hence Weyl-free. Substituting \(m = \tfrac{4\pi}{3}\rho_0 r^3\) gives \(\Psi_2 = 0\) identically and \(\tau_s'(r) = 0\); both verified symbolically.

Put (a) and (b) against Penrose’s Weyl Curvature Hypothesis — Weyl vanishes at initial singularities (low entropy) and is unconstrained at final ones (high entropy) — and the 1939 endpoint lands on the wrong side of the ledger.

The Oppenheimer–Snyder endpoint has the curvature signature of a Big Bang running backwards, not of a gravitational collapse. It is the unique Weyl-free, entropy-minimising member of its family. Oppenheimer and Snyder were right that it is unphysical; the invariant reason is not that dust lacks pressure, but that their endpoint is time-reversed cosmology — reached only by tuning away every degree of freedom that makes collapse collapse.

Every intuition inherited from 1939 — matter crushed to a point of infinite density — is intuition about this one Weyl-free ray. The density does diverge there, as \(\rho \sim s^{-2}\), matter-dominated. Nowhere else does it dominate.

§ 5 — INVERTING THE PREMISE

The obstruction is not a theory of matter

The standard closing line: we can’t say what happens to the matter until we have a theory of matter. Section 4 undercuts the premise. At the generic terminus the matter’s stress-energy is subleading by a full power of \(s\); the dynamics belongs to the vacuum field’s own nonlinearity. Three separable questions have been fused into one.

QQuestionWhat it actually needs
Q1What is the terminal geometry?Vacuum GR. Largely answered: Kasner/BKL, matter subdominant.
Q2What happens to matter’s degrees of freedom?The structure of the causal boundary. Not a matter theory.
Q3Does the terminus occur, or is it replaced?Quantum gravity — but the handover scale is set by curvature, \(s \sim t_{\text{Pl}}\) from \(2/9s^2 \sim \ell_{\text{Pl}}^{-2}\), independent of what fell in.
Established

Where matter genuinely does enter is sharper and far more interesting than “we need a theory of it.” In the cosmological-billiards picture the terminal dynamics is a billiard in the space of Kasner exponents, and matter enters only by determining which walls exist — never as an energy source:

  • Pure 4D gravity → the table is the Weyl chamber of the hyperbolic Kac–Moody algebra \(AE_3\); finite volume, therefore chaotic, with \(u\) iterated by the Gauss map.
  • A massless scalar or stiff fluid (\(p = \rho\), \(\rho \sim s^{-2}\) — the one matter that isn’t subdominant) removes walls and kills the chaos: monotone Kasner.
  • Eleven-dimensional supergravity → \(E_{10}\). Pure gravity in \(D \ge 11\) → non-chaotic.
The terminus does not need a theory of matter. It needs the field content — the spectrum of p-forms and dilaton couplings that fixes which Kac–Moody Weyl chamber you are bouncing in. That is far weaker and far more tractable; and for the energy density it is not a requirement at all.

§ 6 — ROTATING HOLES

The worldline may not end at all

Every astrophysical black hole rotates, and Kerr’s interior is not Schwarzschild’s. The inner Cauchy horizon is destabilised by mass inflation: the Hawking mass diverges exponentially, curvature blows up. But the tidal distortion — the double time-integral of the tidal tensor, which is what actually deforms a body — converges. In Ori’s weak null singularity the metric is continuous but not \(C^1\): tidal forces diverge while their effect on an infalling body stays finite.

Then Dafermos and Luk proved the \(C^0\) formulation of strong cosmic censorship false for Kerr. For data converging to Kerr in the exterior, the maximal development admits a continuous extension across the Cauchy horizon. Censorship is expected to survive in the \(H^1_{\text{loc}}\) sense — the extension exists but fails to be a weak solution.

Penrose guarantees an incomplete geodesic. For rotating black holes that incompleteness sits where the spacetime is continuously extendible — the worldline runs out of this solution, not out of spacetime. “Matter vanishes at the end of its worldline” is not established for any realistic black hole. What is established is that the Einstein equations stop determining the future: a failure of predictivity, not of existence.

This is the sharpest available correction to the received account, and it is not speculative. It is a theorem, and it is about the only black holes that exist.

Reframe
§ 7 — CONFERRAL

“Properties passed to the hole” is a theorem about surface integrals

Why do \(M\), \(Q\), \(J\) survive when nothing else does? The usual answer invokes no-hair. The structural answer is cleaner and does the actual work: \(M\), \(Q\), \(J\) are surface integrals at infinity — ADM, Komar, Gauss. Their conservation is a statement about flux through a two-sphere in the exterior. It never referenced the interior, so nothing at the terminus can threaten it. Nothing is transferred through anything; the bookkeeping closes in a region the matter has already left.

Baryon number has no long-range field, hence no Gauss law, hence no exterior representative — which is the entire content of “black holes violate baryon number.”

Exactly the charges with Gauss laws are conferred. Everything else was never an exterior quantity to begin with. “Conferral” is not a process at the singularity; it is a theorem about which functionals of the interior data are measurable from outside.

Formally, let \(\mu\) be the terminal measure — the pushforward of the matter measure along the congruence onto the causal boundary. The exterior sees only three Gauss-protected moments of it:

Information loss as non-injectivity

\[ \mu \;\longmapsto\; (M,\,Q,\,J) \]

Information loss is the non-injectivity of that map. The fibre over a given \((M,Q,J)\) is a space of measures on the terminal boundary, and Bekenstein–Hawking asserts its log-volume is \(A/4 = 4\pi M^2\) — about \(1.05\times10^{77}\) nats for a solar mass.

§ 8 — THREE CONJECTURES

Blackboard proposals, each with a way to kill it

Clearly labelled: these are proposals, not results. Each is stated with the observation that would end it.

C1

Terminal Measure Program, with an entropy selection principle

Replace the failed b-boundary with the causal boundary of the maximal development, equipped with the pushforward measure \(\mu\), treated as a Lorentzian metric-measure space in the synthetic framework now available — Lorentzian length spaces, timelike Ricci bounds by optimal transport, which already yield synthetic proofs of the Hawking and Penrose theorems. The finite Lorentzian diameter \(\pi M\) from §3 is what makes the completion tractable.

The new part is a requirement: a boundary construction should be obliged to yield a measure space of entropy \(A/4\). No existing construction is asked to do this.

Kill-test — and I expect it to failCompute \(\mu\) for Oppenheimer–Snyder, where everything is explicit. All dust reaches the terminus simultaneously in comoving time; the boundary is an antichain parametrised by \(\chi \in [0,\chi_0]\), with entropy fixed by the initial data — not \(4\pi M^2\). If that is right, the classical terminal boundary is too small to carry Bekenstein–Hawking entropy, which is a clean statement of the information problem in purely geometric terms — and it says the entropy does not live at the terminus.
C2

The silent terminal boundary is an antichain

Under asymptotic silence, particle horizons shrink to zero at the terminus, spatial derivatives drop out, and each spatial point decouples into its own dynamical system. Conjecture: the causal boundary of a generic terminus is an antichain — no two terminal points causally related, zero Lorentzian separation, but carrying a non-trivial measure. Information loss is then not annihilation but causal decoupling: each worldline’s terminal data becomes permanently inaccessible to every other.

Kill-testSilence is known to fail on a measure-zero set — the “spikes,” partially controlled rigorously in recent stable-singularity-formation work. Whether spikes are boundary-relevant is exactly the crux. If they carry positive boundary measure, C2 is dead.
C3

A universal classical information-destruction rate

The BKL parameter is iterated by the Gauss map \(u \mapsto 1/(u-1)\), whose invariant measure is \(du/\bigl((1+u)\ln 2\bigr)\) and whose Kolmogorov–Sinai entropy is

\[ h = \frac{\pi^2}{6\ln 2} = 2.3731\ \text{nats} = 3.4237\ \text{bits per BKL era} \]

Conjecture: this is the rate of classical information destruction at a chaotic terminus — per spatial cell, per era — and it is universal, independent of what collapsed. That universality is the same kind of universality as \(A/4\), which is the whole reason to suspect a relation.

Kill-testCount effective silent cells inside the horizon in the asymptotic regime, multiply by \(h\) per era, cut off at the Planck era. If that reproduces \(4\pi M^2\), the coincidence isn’t one. If it misses by many orders of magnitude — the likely outcome — then C3 describes the classical cascade only; combined with C1’s expected failure it says the entropy lives on the horizon and not at the terminus, which is itself worth establishing.

§ 8b — TWO PROOFS, ADDED AFTER THE NUMERIC COMPANION FOUND THEM

Neither cited, nor merely checked to some decimal places

Everything above this line was either cited (a theorem I did not prove) or checked numerically (an exponent, a ratio, a coincidence to some number of decimal places). Building terminus.py turned up two claims that are neither. Both are stated precisely enough that a wrong step would be falsifiable; neither is claimed as unknown to specialists in this field — only as more than I had actually shown before checking.

Theorem 1 — the BKL “chord” is exact in the Bianchi II limit, not approximate

terminus.py’s bounce-linearity check measured a real Mixmaster trajectory against a straight line joining its flanking Kasner points and found agreement to within \(10^{-4}\) to \(10^{-7}\) — good, but unexplained, reported as an empirical fact with no claim about why.

Restrict to the idealized limit the heuristic actually describes: Bianchi II vacuum, N₂ = N₃ = 0 identically, Ω = 0. The vacuum constraint forces q = 2 − (3/2)N₁², which collapses the equations of motion to:

Bianchi II vacuum, reduced

\[ \frac{d\Sigma_+}{d\tau} = \frac{3}{2}N_1^2(2-\Sigma_+), \qquad \frac{d\Sigma_-}{d\tau} = -\frac{3}{2}N_1^2\,\Sigma_- \]

Divide one by the other: \(N_1^2\) cancels exactly, leaving a first-order ODE in the plane alone, with no \(\tau\)- or \(N_1\)-dependence left:

\[ \frac{d\Sigma_-}{d\Sigma_+} = \frac{-\Sigma_-}{2-\Sigma_+} \]

Separable: \(d(\ln\Sigma_-) = d(\ln(\Sigma_+-2))\).

K := Σ₋/(Σ₊ − 2) is an exact constant of motion of the Bianchi II vacuum flow.

Not to leading order — exactly. Differentiating \(K\) along the actual flow with no small-\(N_1\) truncation anywhere gives \(dK/d\tau \equiv 0\) as a rational-function identity (verify.py, Theorem 1). Every Bianchi II bounce lies exactly on a straight chord of the Kasner circle — and every such chord, extended, passes through the single point \((\Sigma_+,\Sigma_-)=(2,0)\), which sits outside the physical disk (\(4>1\)): the whole one-parameter family of bounces is a pencil of secants through a common external point.

terminus.py confirms this by integrating the reduced 3-variable system directly — so N₂=N₃=0 holds by construction, not by falling below a detection threshold — across five initial conditions, through a full nonlinear bounce (\(N_1\) sweeping from \(\sim 10^{-200}\) to \(O(1)\) and back). \(K\) is conserved to \(10^{-15}\)–\(10^{-18}\): the floor of double-precision arithmetic, not a small residual.

That also retroactively explains the earlier \(10^{-4}\)–\(10^{-7}\): it is not a physical correction from the two “quiescent” axes being merely small rather than zero — checking directly, their squared size during a bounce runs \(10^{-30}\) to \(10^{-170}\), far below the observed residual, so that tidy story is false, and it would have been wrong to assert it without checking. The residual is dominated by something cruder: the original check selects each chord’s endpoint operationally, as whichever sample first drops below a fixed threshold — an \(O(\text{threshold})\) choice about where in the shell to sample, not a feature of the dynamics. Swap the operational proxy for the real reduced system and the residual doesn’t shrink — it disappears to machine precision.

Theorem 2 — the maximal-slicing radius r₀ = 3M/2 is a true extremum, by hand

§2’s limiting cylinder came from asking a computer algebra system to solve \(P(r,C) = r^4 - 2Mr^3 + C^2 = 0\) jointly with \(\partial P/\partial r = 0\). Solid, but a computer asserting a fact is not a proof of one. The fact itself is elementary.

Claim

h(r) := r³(2M − r) attains its maximum on (0, 2M) uniquely at r = 3M/2, where h = 27M⁴/16.

Proof. \(h(0)=h(2M)=0\), and \(h(r)>0\) on the open interval, so the maximum is interior. \(h'(r) = 6Mr^2-4r^3 = 2r^2(3M-2r)\), whose only zero on \(r>0\) is \(r=3M/2\) (the factor \(r^2\) contributes only the boundary point \(r=0\), excluded since \(r>0\)). \(h''(3M/2) = -9M^2 < 0\): a genuine maximum, and the only interior critical point, hence global. Direct substitution: \(h(3M/2) = (3M/2)^3(M/2) = 27M^4/16\). ∎

Since \(P(r,C)=0 \iff C^2=h(r)\), this is exactly the statement that \(C^2=27M^4/16\) is the largest value for which the maximal-slicing radial profile has any real solution at all — the algebraic content of why the family of slices cannot be pushed past \(r_0=3M/2\). (The physical embedding — that boundary-time evolution actually drives the family toward this extremal \(C\), asymptotically, never reaching it — is Estabrook–Wahlquist–Christensen–DeWitt–Smarr–Tsiang 1973 and Reinhart 1973, cited not re-derived; what’s new here is only the hand proof of the extremal-value fact, in place of “a computer algebra system solved it.”)

§ 10 — THE BLACKBOARD IN ONE PARAGRAPH

Each clause weakens under the filter

The received story runs: matter falls in, its worldline ends, its properties pass to the hole, and we cannot say more without a theory of matter. “Its worldline ends” is Penrose’s incompleteness, which for rotating holes sits at a continuously extendible boundary — the equations stop predicting, the spacetime does not stop. “Matter vanishes” is foliation-dependent; in maximal slicing nothing ever vanishes and the collapse never completes. “Properties conferred” is a theorem about Gauss laws in the exterior, not a process at the terminus. And “we need a theory of matter” has it backwards: at the generic terminus the matter’s energy density is subleading by a full power of \(s\), the geometry is Weyl-dominated and profile-independent, and matter enters only by choosing which Kac–Moody Weyl chamber the billiard bounces in. The 1939 endpoint that started all of this is not merely unphysical — it is the unique Weyl-free, entropy-minimising ray in its own family, which is to say a Big Bang played backwards. The generic terminus looks nothing like it, and most of what remains unknown there is a question about the gravitational field, not about what fell in.
